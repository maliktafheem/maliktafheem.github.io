What gets deleted

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. It removes:

  • Your account and profile (email, phone, name, date of birth, country, occupation)
  • All of your financial data — expenses, incomes, budgets, allocations, categories, and LIFE targets
  • Your saved preferences

You can also delete your account from inside the app: Settings → Profile → Danger Zone. This web page exists so you can request deletion even if you no longer have the app installed.

Step 1 — Verify it's you

Enter the email address for your account. We'll send you a secure sign-in link. You can only delete your account after you click that link, which proves you own the email.

We never delete an account from a typed email alone — you must confirm ownership via the emailed link first.

Step 2 — Confirm deletion

Signed in as —

This will permanently delete your account and all of your data. This action is irreversible.

Account deleted
